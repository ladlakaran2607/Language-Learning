# Curriculum A1 → B2

Each unit is one or more `lesson` sessions. Topics in brackets are the vocabulary themes to use in examples, chats and reading.

## A1: survival (about 30 units, roughly 2–3 months at 45 min/day)
1. Alphabet, pronunciation (ä ö ü ß, ei/ie, ch, sch, z, final -d/-b) [greetings]
2. Personal pronouns, *sein*, *haben* [introducing yourself]
3. Present tense of regular verbs [hobbies]
4. Noun gender: der/die/das and gender hints (-ung, -heit, -chen…) [the home]
5. Plurals (the 5 main patterns) [shopping]
6. Basic word order: verb in position 2, yes/no questions, W-questions [daily routine]
7. Nominative vs. accusative; *ein/kein* [food and drink]
8. Negation: *nicht* vs. *kein* [likes and dislikes]
9. Possessive articles (mein, dein, sein, ihr…) [family]
10. Irregular present tense (fahren, essen, lesen, nehmen…) [transport]
11. Modal verbs: können, müssen, wollen, möchten, dürfen, sollen [rules, plans]
12. Separable verbs (aufstehen, einkaufen, anrufen) [the day]
13. Time: clock, days, months, *am/um/im* [appointments]
14. Imperative (du/ihr/Sie) [directions, instructions]
15. Perfekt with *haben* [the weekend]
16. Perfekt with *sein*, irregular participles [travel]
17. Accusative prepositions (für, ohne, gegen, durch, um) [gifts]
18. Dative case: articles and pronouns [helping people]
19. Dative prepositions (mit, nach, bei, seit, von, zu, aus) [the city]
20. Präteritum of *sein, haben*, modal verbs [childhood]
21. Review and A1 mock test (Goethe Start Deutsch 1 format)

## A2: everyday independence (about 3 months)
22. Two-way prepositions (Wechselpräpositionen) + wo?/wohin? [furniture, moving house]
23. Verbs of position: stellen/stehen, legen/liegen, hängen [the flat]
24. Reflexive verbs [routine, health]
25. Coordinating conjunctions: und, aber, oder, denn, sondern [opinions]
26. Subordinate clauses: weil, dass, wenn, ob (verb at the end) [reasons, work]
27. Comparative and superlative [comparing cities and products]
28. Adjective endings I: after the definite article [clothes]
29. Adjective endings II: after the indefinite article or no article [descriptions]
30. Verbs with dative objects (helfen, gefallen, gehören) [preferences]
31. Verbs with fixed prepositions (warten auf, denken an) + da-/wo- compounds [feelings]
32. Konjunktiv II for politeness: würde, hätte, wäre, könnte [requests, wishes]
33. Indirect questions; *zu* + infinitive [bureaucracy, the Bürgeramt]
34. Time clauses: als vs. wenn, bevor, nachdem [life stories]
35. Review and A2 mock test (Goethe A2)

## B1: comfortable conversation (about 4–5 months)
36. Präteritum of regular and irregular verbs (for reading and storytelling) [stories]
37. Plusquamperfekt [narrating the past]
38. Relative clauses: nominative and accusative [describing people and things]
39. Relative clauses: dative, with prepositions, *was/wo* [the workplace]
40. Passive voice, present and past (werden + participle) [processes, news]
41. Futur I and expressing probability [plans, predictions]
42. Konjunktiv II: hypotheticals and the past (hätte gemacht) [regrets, advice]
43. Two-part conjunctions: entweder…oder, sowohl…als auch, weder…noch [pros and cons]
44. Genitive case and genitive prepositions (wegen, trotz, während) [formal writing]
45. Infinitive clauses: um…zu, ohne…zu, statt…zu [goals]
46. N-declension (der Kunde, den Kunden) [people and professions]
47. Discourse markers for opinions and arguments [debating]
48. Formal email and letter conventions [complaints, applications]
49. Review and B1 mock test (Goethe B1 or telc B1)

## B2: fluency and range (about 5–6 months)
50. Passive with modal verbs; *sein*-passive (Zustandspassiv) [technology]
51. Passive alternatives: man, sich lassen, -bar, sein + zu [rules and regulations]
52. Konjunktiv I and indirect speech [reporting news]
53. Participles used as adjectives (der lachende Mann, das gekaufte Auto) [press texts]
54. Extended participial phrases [academic and official German]
55. Nominalisation vs. verbal style [formal writing]
56. Advanced connectors: dennoch, folglich, indem, sodass, je…desto [essays]
57. Modal particles: doch, mal, ja, eben, halt, wohl [sounding natural]
58. Verb-noun phrases (Funktionsverbgefüge): eine Entscheidung treffen [business]
59. Idioms and colloquial speech; register switching [film, TV]
60. Review and B2 mock test (Goethe B2)

Throughout B1–B2: one chat on a current-affairs topic each week, one essay each week, and extended reading of real texts (news, short books).
